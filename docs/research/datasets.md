# Datasets

## Planned datasets
- Adult
- ACSIncome
- South German Credit
- CrowS-Pairs
- BBQ
- BharatBBQ
- SB-Bench

## Notes
- Adult and ACSIncome are used for tabular fairness demos.
- South German Credit supports the lending demo path.
- BBQ, BharatBBQ, CrowS-Pairs, and SB-Bench support language-bias checks.
- Dataset licensing and access terms must be checked before publishing raw
  data. The repo keeps raw and processed dataset folders ignored except for
  `.gitkeep` markers.
